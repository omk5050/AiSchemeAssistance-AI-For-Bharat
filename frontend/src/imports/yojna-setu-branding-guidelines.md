Design branding and navigation for a modern civic technology platform called:

YojnaSetu

Tagline suggestion:

“Connecting Citizens to Government Welfare Schemes”

This platform helps citizens discover government welfare schemes they qualify for using rule-based eligibility evaluation combined with AI explanations.

The design should feel like a modern government digital service platform, not a startup SaaS website.

The branding must feel:

• trustworthy
• professional
• citizen-friendly
• modern but simple

Logo Design

Create a minimal modern logo for YojnaSetu.

The logo should visually represent:

• connection between citizens and welfare schemes
• government support
• AI-assisted eligibility discovery

Logo Concept

Combine three symbolic elements in the logo.

1. Bridge / Connection

Represents a bridge between citizens and government schemes.

2. Government Pillars

Represents official welfare programs and government support.

3. AI Network

Small digital nodes representing AI intelligence.
