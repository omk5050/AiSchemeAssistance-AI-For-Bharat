Create a modern, professional government-style web application UI for a project called:

“AI Scheme Eligibility Assistant”

The application helps citizens discover government welfare schemes they qualify for using a rule-based eligibility engine and AI explanations.

The design should look professional, trustworthy, and government-grade, but still modern and accessible.

Avoid generic AI-style card layouts and avoid startup SaaS templates.
Instead focus on structured layouts, clear information hierarchy, and credibility similar to high-quality government portals.

The UI should feel transparent, simple, and trustworthy.

Target Users

Primary users are:

• Citizens checking welfare scheme eligibility
• Farmers
• Students
• Unemployed youth
• Retired citizens

The UI must be simple and understandable even for non-technical users.

Design Principles

Follow these rules strictly:

• Clean, structured layouts
• High readability
• Professional government tone
• Minimal clutter
• Accessible design
• Clear hierarchy of information

Avoid:

❌ Generic SaaS card grids
❌ Startup-style landing pages
❌ Overly colorful designs
❌ Dark futuristic UI

Prefer:

✅ Section based layouts
✅ Timeline or list style components
✅ Clear content blocks
✅ Subtle gradients
✅ Professional typography
✅ Government portal style layouts
